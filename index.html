About Us

Udacity is a leading online learning platform that offers courses, nanodegrees, and certifications in various fields such as technology, business, and data science. Our mission is to provide high-quality education to anyone, anywhere, and at any time.

Our courses are designed by industry experts and are regularly updated to reflect the latest trends and technologies.

A Brief History

Udacity was founded in 2011 by Sebastian Thrun, David Stavens, and Mike Sokolsky. The company was originally called Udacity, Inc. and was later renamed to Udacity, Inc. in 2012.

Since then, Udacity has grown to become one of the most popular online learning platforms in the world, with over 3 million students enrolled in our courses.
